Rules of the Hall

Unfiltered conversation, dark humor, loud games, questionable decisions, and good people. Speak freely, take a joke, and keep the chaos worth coming back to.

🔞 R-rated atmosphere 🗣️ Speak freely ⚡ Controlled chaos
ᚷ ᛉ ᛏ
Rule 01

🗣️ Speak freely. Own the impact.

This is not a family-friendly server. Swearing, strong opinions, arguments, trash talk, and offensive jokes are allowed. You are free to speak—but nobody is entitled to relentlessly target, stalk, threaten, or dogpile another member.

Rule 02

😂 R-rated humor is welcome

Dark, crude, and politically incorrect humor is part of the Hall. Context matters. Jokes are not the same as credible threats, targeted harassment, or trying to make one person miserable every time they show up.

Rule 03

🎮 Keep the Hall organized

Use channels for their intended purpose. Post game news, friend codes, clips, memes, and links where they belong so people can find what they came for.

Rule 04

🚫 No spam, scams, or drive-by promotion

No malicious links, mass mentions, repeated posts, unsolicited DMs, or advertising without permission. Share your work only in spaces made for it.

Rule 05

🔒 Protect people’s privacy

Do not post private conversations, personal information, addresses, or identifying details without permission. What happens in a private conversation stays private.

Rule 06

⚔️ Mods use judgment, not scripts

Context, intent, patterns, and severity all matter. Moderators may step in when behavior is wrecking the room, even if no sentence here describes it word for word. Appeal a decision privately instead of derailing the server.

Rule 07

🎙️ Voice channels have different energy

Joining voice just to chill is always welcome. Don’t blast audio, talk over everyone nonstop, stream disturbing content, or ruin a focused game session.

🌿 Smoking Lounge Casual conversation, background company, laughs, and low-pressure hanging out.
🎯 Gaming Room For squads, callouts, multiplayer sessions, and anyone trying to lock in.
Rule 08

📜 Keep it legal and inside Discord’s rules

No doxxing, scams, malware, credible threats, sexual content involving minors, or other illegal material. Discord’s Terms of Service and Community Guidelines still apply here. Free speech does not mean putting the server or its members at risk.

⚡ The short version

Say what you mean. Take what you dish out. Don’t threaten people, expose their private information, scam them, or make yourself the entire server’s problem.
